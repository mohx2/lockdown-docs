\# Privacy



Lockdown is designed to run locally on your Windows PC. Your focus sessions, allowlist, and settings are stored on your device.



\## What Lockdown stores



Lockdown saves the following information locally so your settings and sessions can persist:



\- Allowed applications

\- Allowed websites

\- Your last selected session duration

\- Your current session's end time

\- Your emergency exit count



This information is stored in the application's local data folder.



\## Website Blocking



Lockdown uses a Chrome extension to handle website blocking during focus sessions. The extension communicates with the desktop application locally to check your current session and allowed websites.



\## Data Collection



Lockdown does not require an account to use.



It is designed to work without collecting personal information or sending your focus session data to an external server.



\## Your Data



Your settings are stored locally on your device. You can remove them by deleting Lockdown's local data files.



\## Questions



If you have any questions or concerns about privacy, feel free to reach out through the \[official website](https://lockdowned.pages.dev).
